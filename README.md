A feature dramatic screenplay climactic sequence set in the high desert of **Sedona, Arizona**, unfolding during the exact real-world peak of the **Perseid meteor shower on the night of August 12–13, 2024**.

---

## 🎬 Project Overview

- **Title:** *The Swift-Tuttle Window*
- **Genre:** Science Drama / Emotional Reconciliation
- **Setting:** Schnebly Hill Vista overlooking Cathedral Rock and Bell Rock, Sedona, Arizona (Elevation: ~6,000 ft / Dark Sky Community).
- **Core Characters:**
  - **Dr. Maya Lin (36):** Planetary astronomer and meteoroid dynamicist at Lowell Observatory (Flagstaff, AZ). Analytical, hyper-focused, carrying unresolved grief.
  - **Eli Lin (67):** Maya's estranged father, a retired aerospace optical technician living off-grid outside Sedona. Struggling with failing memory and seeking closure.
- **Premise:** Against the dramatic red-rock landscape of Sedona, Maya and Eli camp on an isolated sandstone ridge to catch the peak of the Perseid meteor shower. As an Arizona summer monsoon cloud deck threatens to obscure the heavens, the clearing sky, the 11:40 PM moonset, and the pre-dawn bombardment of ancient comet dust become the canvas for their unspoken truth.

## 🔭 Scientific & Meteorological Grounding

This screenplay is engineered with precise observational astrophysics and historical meteorological records for Sedona, Arizona:

1. **Astrophysics of the Perseids:**
   - **Parent Body:** Comet 109P/Swift-Tuttle (a massive 26 km diameter icy nucleus with an eccentric 133-year orbit).
   - **Atmospheric Entry Velocity:** 59 km/s (~132,000 mph).
   - **Radiant Point:** Constellation Perseus (Right Ascension ~03h 10m, Declination +57°), rising in the northeast and climbing toward the zenith by 4:00 AM.
   - **Visual Manifestations:** Fast white-hot streaks, emerald-green persistent ionization trains (excited forbidden oxygen lines [O I] at 557.7 nm and vaporized magnesium/nickel), and luminous fireball bolides.

2. **Astronomy Data (Sedona, AZ — August 12–13, 2024):**
   - **Sunset:** 7:18 PM MST
   - **Moonrise:** 1:48 PM MST
   - **Moonset:** 11:40 PM MST (Crucial dramatic pivot: First-quarter moon at 47%–61% illumination drops behind the crimson western bluffs, creating true Bortle Class 2/3 dark-sky conditions).
   - **Astronomical Twilight & Peak Window:** 01:00 AM – 04:30 AM MST.
   - **Sunrise:** 5:46 AM MST

3. **Historical Weather Data (Sedona, AZ — August 12–13, 2024):**
   - **Late Afternoon Monsoon:** 4:00 PM – 6:30 PM (Patchy rain, gusty SW winds up to 18 mph, 88% cloud cover, 0.5 mm precipitation).
   - **Early Evening:** 8:00 PM – 11:00 PM (Lingering overcast stratocumulus, 66–68°F, building character anxiety).
   - **Post-Midnight Clearing:** Abrupt clearing at 01:00 AM (cloud cover drops to 15%), followed by pristine windows from 03:00 AM to 04:30 AM (14% cloud cover, 70°F, light NE winds at 4 mph).
